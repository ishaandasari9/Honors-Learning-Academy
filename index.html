Why families trust us

Careful with your kid, honest about the rest.

Tutor standards

  • Approved before matched. No tutor meets a student until we have reviewed and approved their application.

  • They teach only what they know cold. Tutors list the subjects they are confident in and we match to that list.

  • Every session goes on the record. Date, subject, and hours, logged in the portal.

  • We collect only what we need. Names and an email to make the match, nothing sold, nothing shared.

  • A real person answers. Email a founder and you get a reply.

How we handle your information

Genuinely free

Private tutoring runs $40 to $80 an hour. Ours is free, and always will be. No fees, no upsells, no paid tiers.

On the record

Tutors log every session, so volunteer hours and your student's progress are both tracked.

Now the practical questions.

Does my student have to be struggling?
No. Some students come to catch up. Others come to get ahead, whether that's AP prep, a specialized track, or staying sharp over a break.
How often do sessions happen?
As often as it helps. Once you're matched, you and your tutor set the schedule between you, and you can change it as the term goes on.
What if the tutor isn't the right fit?
Tell us and we'll match you with someone else. No awkward conversation, and no cost either way.
Can we get help right before a test?
Yes, and families do. Just know a match takes a few days, so the earlier you ask, the more time your tutor has to actually prepare.
